INTERVIEW PREP

Prepare by experience, not just topic.

Practice explanation, trade-offs and real situations — not only definitions.

0–1 YEAR

Foundation

  • Introduce yourself as an analyst.
  • INNER vs LEFT JOIN.
  • Primary key and foreign key.
  • Pivot Table use cases.
  • Power BI measure.
  • Walk through one project.
1–3 YEARS

Problem Solving

  • Second-highest salary.
  • CTE vs subquery.
  • Dashboard validation.
  • Relationship design.
  • Explain CALCULATE.
  • Describe a difficult data issue.
3–5 YEARS

Advanced Analyst

  • Optimize a slow query.
  • Design a sales star schema.
  • Handle conflicting definitions.
  • Power Query vs DAX choice.
  • KPI framework.
  • Explain a failed analysis.
5+ YEARS

Senior Scenarios

  • Redesign a slow enterprise model.
  • Metric governance.
  • Challenge unclear requirements.
  • Architecture trade-offs.
  • Review another analyst's work.
  • Communicate uncertainty.
SELF-INTRO FRAMEWORK

Background → tools → problem → your contribution → result → learning.

Avoid listing technologies without showing what you actually did and why the approach mattered.
